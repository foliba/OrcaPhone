Phorca turns your phone or tablet into a complete slicer for FDM 3D printers. Import a model, tweak the settings with your thumb, slice it right on the device, check every layer and send the G-code to your printer – no computer needed.

PREPARE
• Import STL, 3MF and OBJ files – from your files, from other apps via "Open with" / "Share", or by pasting a Printables model link
• 3MF projects from OrcaSlicer, Bambu Studio and MakerWorld: multiple objects, holes (negative volumes), support blockers and enforcers
• Rotate, scale, lay flat on a face, arrange automatically
• Instances: place copies of a model or fill the whole bed, spaced so brims and supports don't touch
• Multiple beds: what doesn't fit goes to the next bed; slice each bed on its own
• Auto cut: parts too big for your printer are split into pieces with dovetail joints
• Your printer's own bed shown as in OrcaSlicer, with an X/Y/Z axis indicator

SLICING ON THE DEVICE
• Walls, top and bottom shells, ironing, seams
• Infill: grid, rectilinear, triangles, cubic, gyroid, concentric
• Supports: organic tree, tree and normal, with blockers and enforcers
• Skirt, brim, vase mode, elephant-foot compensation
• G-code for Marlin, Klipper and RepRapFirmware with print time and filament estimate
• Pressure advance per filament, and a pressure advance calibration tower

PREVIEW
• See every layer and every move in 3D, coloured by feature or speed
• Save or share the G-code

PRINT
• Send to OctoPrint, Klipper (Moonraker, Mainsail, Fluidd) and PrusaLink on your network
• Live status, temperatures, pause, resume and cancel

PROFILES
• Printer, filament and process profiles for more than 400 printer models, from OrcaSlicer's profile library
• New OrcaSlicer profiles are offered when OrcaSlicer publishes them – no app update needed
• Optional: sign in with your OrcaSlicer account to sync your own presets with OrcaSlicer on your computer

Phones and tablets, portrait and landscape, light and dark mode.
