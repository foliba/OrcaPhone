## 0.5.2 — new OrcaSlicer profiles without an app update

### New
- **Newer OrcaSlicer profiles at start-up.** When OrcaSlicer publishes a new stable release, Phorca asks
  whether to download its printer, filament and process profiles and bed graphics (about 2 MB):
  **Download** or **Ignore**, with *Don't ask again*. Presets you already added are not changed.
  The profiles are converted and checked by Phorca's own workflow before the app offers them.
- **Setup → OrcaSlicer profiles** shows which OrcaSlicer version's profiles are in use, switches the
  start-up check on or off, and has **Check now**.
- The **Device** tab is now called **Setup** (same icon).

## 0.5.1 — landscape, axis indicator

### New
- **Axis indicator** at the bed origin (front left corner), as in OrcaSlicer: X red, Y green, Z blue.
  Shows where front, right and up are while you turn the view. In Prepare and Preview.
- **The printer's own bed**, as in OrcaSlicer: the plate with its shape (rounded corners, tabs) and the
  manufacturer's markings and logo, for the 404 printer models OrcaSlicer 2.5.2 has graphics for. Built
  into the app (about 1.7 MB, bed models in a compact format), so it works offline. Printers without
  graphics and custom printers keep the plain grid.
- **Tablets in landscape: one bottom bar instead of two.** Prepare / Preview / Device move into the middle of
  each screen's bar: Prepare keeps its tools on the left and Slice/Re-slice on the right, Preview its file
  buttons on the left and Print on the right, Device has Add printer on the right. Phones and tablets in
  portrait keep the navigation bar.

### Fixes
- In landscape (tablets, phones on their side) the view buttons on the right slid under the object
  panel. On wide screens they now have a column of their own on the right; on narrow screens they share
  the height with the top bar and the panels and can't run into them. They show names when there is room,
  icons only when it's tight, and use two or three columns when even that doesn't fit.
- GitHub Actions workflows moved to their Node 24 versions (Node 20 deprecation warning).
- The device tests run on Android 11, 15, 16 and 17, each on a phone and on a Pixel Tablet emulator
  (landscape and portrait). Espresso 3.7.0 for Android 17 (older versions call a method Android 17 removed).

## 0.5.0 — Phorca

### Renamed: OrcaPhone is now **Phorca**
- New app name and package (`io.github.foliba.phorca`), ready for Google Play. Android treats it as a
  **new app**: install Phorca next to OrcaPhone, sign in to Orca Cloud again (your cloud presets come
  back), then uninstall OrcaPhone. Local-only presets and printer connections are not carried over.
- G-code says `generated by Phorca`; Mainsail/Fluidd read it as before.
- Builds for Google Play: targets Android 16 (API 36), release builds produce an App Bundle (.aab)
  next to the APK, and are signed with the upload key when it is configured.

### 3MF volumes
- **Negative volumes** from Bambu Studio / OrcaSlicer 3MF projects are cut out of their part (holes,
  recesses), instead of being dropped.
- **Support blockers and enforcers** from 3MF projects are kept with their object: no support is
  generated inside a blocker, and overhangs inside an enforcer always get support, also when support is
  switched off (as in OrcaSlicer). Works with normal, tree and organic support.
- In the 3D view the volumes show see-through over the part: negative volumes grey, blockers red,
  enforcers blue. The object panel lists them. They move, turn and scale with the object and its instances.
- **Modifiers** (per-region settings) are not supported yet: importing a file with modifiers now says
  how many were ignored.

### Notes
- Bambu's "endless" support blockers are cut to the part's height so they can be drawn and sliced.
- Auto cut parts don't keep the volumes; you are told when that happens.

## 0.4.2 — Bambu Studio / MakerWorld 3MF

### Fixes
- 3MF files from Bambu Studio and MakerWorld failed with "3MF has no model". They store their thumbnails
  in a way Android's streaming ZIP reader can't get past; 3MF files are now read through the ZIP
  directory like any unzip tool does.
- Parts of a 3MF project that are not printed (support blockers and enforcers, modifiers, negative
  volumes) are no longer merged into the model as solid geometry. A support blocker made one part
  astronomically tall.
- Objects from Bambu Studio / OrcaSlicer projects get their names from the project ("Kopf", "Arm rechts")
  instead of the file name with a number.
- The view buttons are hidden while the plate is empty; they sat behind the "Your plate is empty" card.

## 0.4.1 — instances

### New
- **Instances** (replaces Copy in the object panel): enter how many instances of the model the bed should
  hold, or tap **Fill bed**. Instances are laid out on a grid around the bed centre, never on other
  objects, with enough room between them for two brims side by side and for tree/organic support feet,
  and along the bed edge for the skirt. The sheet shows how many fit; asking for more places that many
  and says so. Instances are never put on another bed.
- Rotating, scaling or laying flat one instance applies to every instance of the model. When instances
  would then overlap or reach off the bed they are laid out again; if some no longer fit, you are told.
  Moving, centring and deleting stay per instance.
- **Model list** (new button next to Add): every model on the bed once, with a ×N badge for its
  instances. Tap a row to select the model. The object panel also shows the ×N badge.
- The view buttons on the right of the 3D view have their names underneath (3D view, Top, Front, Fit,
  Overhang), like the tool buttons in the object panel.

## 0.4.0 — OrcaSlicer 2.5.2

### Printer library
- The OrcaSlicer printer library (Add from OrcaSlicer library, and the base for Orca Cloud presets) is
  updated to OrcaSlicer 2.5.2: 12 new printers (BenFab 450, Qidi X-Smart 5, Sovol SV08 MAX 0.2/1.0 mm),
  265 new filaments (QIDI, PrintaLot, Generic, 3D-Fuel, HATCHBOX, Bambu Lab, Polymaker, Overture, …),
  37 new process presets, and corrected settings for 45 printers (Flashforge default processes and start
  G-code, Prusa XL deretraction speed, Sovol SV08 per-nozzle default processes, Snapmaker U1, …).
  Presets you already added are not changed.

### Orca Cloud
- A preset that was deleted in Orca Cloud and then created again on the phone is uploaded again
  (force push, as OrcaSlicer 2.4+ does) instead of staying on the phone only.
- Switched-off filament overrides are uploaded explicitly so they stay off (matches OrcaSlicer 2.4.2;
  OrcaPhone already did this, now covered by a test).

### Slicing
- Tree and organic supports resting on the model keep the support Z distance above it instead of
  fusing to the part (OrcaSlicer 2.4.0 fixed the same for its tree supports).
- G-code: new `; estimated first layer printing time (normal mode)` line (the first layer only, as fixed
  in OrcaSlicer 2.5.2); start G-code can use `chamber_temperature` and `chamber_minimal_temperature`.
- Arrange, Move to bed and Auto cut leave room along the bed edges for the skirt and brim, and keep
  parts far enough apart for their brims.

## 0.3.1 — print time and thumbnails in Mainsail/Fluidd

### Fixes
- Mainsail/Fluidd (Moonraker) showed no slicer, no estimated print time and no filament use for
  OrcaPhone G-code: the "generated by" line used a date format Moonraker didn't recognise
  (it expects `YYYY-MM-DD at HH:MM:SS`). Moonraker now identifies OrcaPhone and reads the estimated
  time, filament length/weight, layer height and temperatures. Older Moonraker versions are covered
  by an OrcaSlicer-compatible identification line.
- G-code now contains preview thumbnails (300×300 and 32×32 PNG, the models in the filament colour),
  shown in the Mainsail/Fluidd file list and job status and on printers that display them.

## 0.3.0 — auto cut

### Auto cut
- Parts that are too big for the printer get a *Too big for the bed · Auto cut* chip. Auto cut first
  asks which side the part should lie on: drag to turn it, flat faces are shown in different colours,
  tap one to choose.
- The part is then cut on a regular grid so every piece fits the bed with at least 10 mm free on each
  side (and the printer's height). Every cut gets a dovetail joint (tail and slot with 0.2 mm clearance),
  placed where there is solid material; X/Y joints are vertical so pieces slide together from above.
- Each piece goes on its own bed. Cut parts are closed (watertight) meshes and slice normally.

## 0.2.0 — Printables import and multiple beds

Import models straight from Printables links and spread big jobs across several beds.

### Import from Printables
- Paste a printables.com model link (Add → From Printables link). The app lists the model's STL and
  3MF files with Printables' preview images, file sizes, author and licence; pick files and import.
- Downloads show progress and can be cancelled; downloads over 50 MB on mobile data ask first.
- 3MF files are imported as geometry only for now; full 3MF support is coming soon. Other file
  types (STEP, ZIP, G-code, …) are skipped, and a model without STL files explains why.
- Importing onto a non-empty plate asks whether to replace or add; parts larger than the build
  volume ask whether to scale down or keep their size.

### Multiple beds
- Imports, copies and Arrange put objects that don't fit onto additional beds.
- Bed switcher with chips, ‹ › arrows and an n/m indicator on Prepare and Preview.
- Add and delete beds, move objects between beds, arrange all beds or only the current one.
- Slicing works per bed; each bed keeps its own result, time and G-code file.

### Notes
- Printables has no official public API; the app uses the GraphQL API the Printables website
  uses, anonymously (no Printables/Prusa login). Paid models can't be imported.

### Testing
- Device tests now run on Android 11 and Android 15 emulators, including a UI test that imports
  a real Printables model and checks the Import button stays on screen.
- A non-blocking CI check talks to the live Printables API so changes on their side show up early.

## 0.1.0 — first preview

First public preview of OrcaPhone, a native Android slicer inspired by OrcaSlicer.

### Slicing
- Kotlin slicing engine: walls with seam control, top/bottom shells, ironing, six infill patterns
  (grid, rectilinear, triangles, cubic, gyroid, concentric), skirt/brim, spiral vase mode
- Supports: **organic tree supports (default)**, tree supports and normal (grid) supports, with
  interface roofs and "build plate only"
- G-code for Marlin, Klipper and RepRapFirmware with retraction, Z-hop, acceleration control,
  volumetric speed limit, cooling (fan + minimum layer time), M73 progress and a look-ahead time estimate

### App
- Prepare: 3D plate with touch controls, STL/3MF/OBJ import (also via "Open with"/"Share"),
  rotate, scale, lay flat, duplicate, auto-arrange, overhang highlighting
- Presets for printer, filament and process with OrcaSlicer-like saving; quick settings sheet;
  OrcaSlicer printer library with 1,600+ printers
- Preview: 3D toolpaths by feature or speed, layer and move sliders, print time/filament/cost summary
- Device: OctoPrint, Klipper (Moonraker) and PrusaLink — status, upload, start/pause/resume/cancel
- Orca Cloud: sign in with an OrcaSlicer account, two-way sync of printer/filament/process presets,
  cloud printer status, sync diagnostics
- Light/dark theme (or follow the system)

### Known limitations
- Not a port of OrcaSlicer's C++ engine; no bridge detection, Arachne walls, multi-material or variable layer height
- OrcaSlicer G-code scripting (`{if …}`) is not evaluated — check start G-code imported from OrcaSlicer presets
- Printing through Orca Cloud and Bambu Lab network printing are not supported
- The release APK is signed with a debug key (fine for sideloading, not for the Play Store)
